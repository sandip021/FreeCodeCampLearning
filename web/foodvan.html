About Us

Junction Food Van is a modern street food truck serving freshly prepared meals inspired by global flavours. Our focus is on quality ingredients, fast service, and vibrant taste combinations.

Our brand colours — yellow, green, and white — represent freshness, energy, and cleanliness.
