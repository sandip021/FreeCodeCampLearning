Local Cat elected Mayor after promising “MORE NAPS, LESS MEETINGS”
Town embraces unconventional leadership as feline candidate wins hearts and votes

By Staff Reporter

In an unprecedented turn of events, the small town of Willow Creek has elected a three-year-old tabby cat named Whiskers as its new mayor. The landslide victory came after Whiskers ran a surprisingly effective campaign centred on shorter workdays, increased snack breaks, and a strict “no unnecessary meetings” policy.

Residents say they were initially amused by the campaign, which began as a joke on social media. However, Whiskers quickly gained popularity after attending a local town hall meeting—where he reportedly sat silently, blinked slowly, and knocked a glass of water off the table. Many voters described this as “more productive than previous discussions.”

“I’ve never felt more represented,” said local resident Mary Collins. “He listens, he doesn’t interrupt, and he’s very clear about his priorities—mainly food and sleep. Honestly, it’s refreshing.”

Whiskers’ campaign team, consisting of two teenagers and a retired librarian, credited their success to a simple strategy: “We didn’t overpromise. We just promised vibes.”

Since taking office, Mayor Whiskers has implemented several new initiatives, including mandatory afternoon naps, a ban on early morning emails, and the installation of cardboard boxes in all public offices. Critics have raised concerns about his lack of formal qualifications, but supporters argue that his leadership style is “low-stress and highly relatable.”

Political analysts are already calling this a potential shift in governance models. “If this works,” said one expert, “we may see more animals entering politics. There’s already talk of a golden retriever running for council in a neighbouring town.”

As for Mayor Whiskers, he remains focused on his duties—or at least as focused as one can be while chasing a laser pointer during official working hours.

When asked for comment, he declined to respond, choosing instead to nap on a stack of important-looking documents
